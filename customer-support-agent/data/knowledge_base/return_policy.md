# Return & Refund Policy

## Standard Return Window
Customers may request a return or refund within **30 days** of the delivery date.
Items returned after 30 days are not eligible for a standard refund and must be
escalated to a human agent for a case-by-case decision.

## Auto-Approved Refunds
Refunds for orders totaling **$200 or less** that fall within the 30-day window
and have not already been refunded may be **automatically approved** by the
support agent without human review.

## Refunds Requiring Human Escalation
The following must always be escalated to a human agent and must NOT be
auto-approved:
- Orders over $200, regardless of how recent the purchase was.
- Orders outside the 30-day return window.
- Orders that have already been refunded once.
- Requests involving damaged, counterfeit, or missing items (these may involve
  fraud review).
- Any customer who is dissatisfied with the automated decision and asks to
  speak to a human.

## Non-Returnable Items
Digital downloads, gift cards, and clearance items marked "Final Sale" are not
eligible for return under any circumstances and should always be escalated if
the customer disputes this.

## Refund Method
Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method and typically take
5-10 business days to appear on the customer's statement.
