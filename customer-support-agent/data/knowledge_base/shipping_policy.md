# Shipping Policy

## Processing Time
Orders are processed within 1-2 business days before shipment.

## Shipping Speeds
- Standard Shipping: 5-7 business days, free on orders over $50.
- Express Shipping: 2-3 business days, flat fee of $12.99.
- Overnight Shipping: 1 business day, flat fee of $29.99.

## Delayed or Lost Packages
If a package has not arrived within 3 business days of the estimated delivery
date, the customer support agent should first check the order status. If the
carrier tracking shows the package as lost or if delivery is more than 7 days
late, this must be escalated to a human agent for a replacement or refund
decision, since it may require carrier claims.

## International Shipping
International orders may be subject to customs fees which are the
responsibility of the customer. International shipping delays of any length
should be escalated if the customer is requesting compensation.
