# Warranty Policy

## Standard Warranty
All products purchased directly from us carry a 1-year limited warranty
against manufacturing defects.

## What's Covered
- Manufacturing defects present at time of purchase
- Premature hardware failure under normal use

## What's Not Covered
- Accidental damage, water damage, or misuse
- Cosmetic wear and tear
- Damage caused by unauthorized repairs or modifications

## Warranty Claims
All warranty claims (as opposed to standard 30-day returns) must be escalated
to a human agent, since they require physical inspection of the product or
photo evidence review. The support agent should never approve a warranty
replacement automatically.
